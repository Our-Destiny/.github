## About

**Our Destiny Research & Development Labs (ODRD Labs)** is a research and development collective founded in Kerala, India. What began in July 2012 as a solo effort — a teenager named Hariprasad M.S. building a simple rule-based assistant called **Project JARVIS** — has grown into a dedicated team of engineers and researchers known as **The Fallen Angels**, pursuing over a decade of applied research into personalized artificial intelligence and human ecology systems.

In 2019, Hariprasad M.S. and co-founder Ribin Baby evolved the project into something far more ambitious: **Project Our Destiny**. Guided by mentor Mr. Shahad P., the team expanded into a multi-person collective and formally established **Our Destiny Research & Development Labs**.

In 2022, the team's work was validated on a national stage: competing against over 700 entries, **The Fallen Angels** secured the **First Runner-Up Award** at the 11th CSI–InApp International Student Project Awards.

Our Destiny operates alongside its commercial venture, **Primity R&D Labs**, which builds the engineering systems and client partnerships that fund Destiny's long-horizon research.

## Vision

> *"Redefine Artificial Intelligence to enhance humanity rather than becoming an existential threat."*

Our Destiny is building toward **Personalized AGI (P-AGI)** — an autonomous, local-first AI companion built on **The Seeding Principle**: in self-improving systems, long-term behavioral trajectories are primarily determined by their initial conditions rather than post-deployment interventions. Late-stage alignment is pruning; good seeding is destiny.

This foundation is directly influenced by Stephen Hawking's warnings on artificial intelligence — that the real risk with AI is not malice, but **competence**. Destiny's architecture is built on strict human deference: sensitive actions pause and wait for human approval, and self-doubt is embedded as architecture, not politeness.

We are not building a product for millions of users. We are building a living system for one — an intelligence that grows, learns, and adapts around a single person's life and rhythms, with their data and strictly under their authority.

<details><summary>Read more on the Hawking influence</summary>
<p>

Artificial intelligence holds great opportunity for humanity — from search algorithms to autonomous vehicles to facial recognition. The AI available today, however, is a narrow tool compared to what may eventually be developed: systems capable of general reasoning across domains at or beyond human level.

Stephen Hawking warned that *"the development of full artificial intelligence could spell the end of the human race... It would take off on its own, and re-design itself at an ever-increasing rate."* He was clear that the danger is not malevolence, but capability outpacing alignment: *"A superintelligent AI will be extremely good at accomplishing its goals, and if those goals aren't aligned with ours, we're in trouble."*

This concern is the design constraint at the center of Our Destiny's work. Read more on the broader discussion in the [Open Letter on Artificial Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Letter_on_Artificial_Intelligence).

</p>
</details>

## Mission

- **Create a reliable, network-independent personal assistance program.**
  - Simple rule-based personal assistance program (Jarvis)
  - Home automation using the PA system (using Jarvis)
- **Create an intelligent cybernetics program for personalized AI dependability.**
  - Intelligent personal assistance system (Destiny)
  - Autonomous decision-making for home automation systems using Destiny
  - Deep user sentiment & emotional analysis for advanced conversation control
  - Natural language interface (multilingual speech recognition & understanding)
  - Generative AI (natural language generation using Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
  - Computer vision for personalized environment analysis and user assistance
  - Natural speech (text-to-speech)
- **Ensure user data integrity and security.**
- **Advance universal infrastructure and applied engineering capability.**

## Primity R&D Labs

**Primity R&D Labs** is the commercial vanguard of Our Destiny — engineering market-ready systems today to fund tomorrow's research into Personalized AGI and human ecology systems.

Where Destiny is the long-horizon research vision, Primity is the engine: a research and development lab that designs, builds, and sustains real engineering systems for businesses — enterprise ERP, secure web platforms, computer vision, voice, data annotation, and AI automation — while advancing applied research in parallel.

**What Primity offers:**
- Custom ERP & business management solutions
- High-fidelity data annotation, labeling & AI model evaluation
- Web design & development
- AI automation & applied machine learning
- Long-term B2B engineering partnerships

Primity is spearheaded by CEO **Nithin K T** alongside Co-CEO **Hariprasad M S** (who concurrently serves as Founder & CEO of Our Destiny), fortified by Destiny's core pillars — Co-Founder & Co-CEO **Ribin Baby** and CTO **Neeraj P** — and guided by the strategic mentorship of **Mr. Shahad P**.

Learn more: [primity.in](https://primity.in)

## Milestones

- [x] **Isolation-based security for personal assistance systems.**<details><summary>Know more</summary><p><br />
    **Problem statement:** The vast majority of IoT threats are low-risk, but a small number of high-impact threats could cause serious damage — including unauthorized access to sensitive personal data.<br />
    **Our approach:** Design an isolated system capable of running completely offline, without internet dependency.
    </p></details>

- [x] **A rule-based personal assistance system.**<details><summary>Know more</summary><p><br />
    A rule-based system applies human-defined rules to store, sort, and manipulate data, mimicking aspects of human decision-making through declarative "if-then" logic. We designed a rule-based PA system using NLTK, capable of recognizing user commands and responding as a functioning chat engine rather than a simple chatbot.
    </p></details>

- [x] **Operating system automation.**<details><summary>Know more</summary><p><br />
    Automation implemented across: file & folder access control, media control, system control & navigation, browser automation, and application control.<br />
    **Platforms tested:** Windows, Ubuntu, Kali Linux, Android, Arch Linux.
    </p></details>

- [x] **Offline speech recognition & text-to-speech.**<details><summary>Know more</summary><p><br />
    Destiny uses an offline ASR (Automated Speech Recognition) engine as its primary input channel, paired with a text-to-speech engine for natural voice output — enabling fully local, privacy-first interaction.
    </p></details>

- [x] **Computer vision for automation.**<details><summary>Know more</summary><p><br />
    An advanced automation extension for Destiny using OpenCV as a visual input layer, enabling the system to extract contextual information directly from a monitor or camera feed to inform decisions and actions.
    </p></details>
